Research Interests

Bose–Einstein Condensates

Mathematical and numerical studies on Bogoliubov–de Gennes excitations, ground states and stationary states.

Eigenvalue Problems

Numerical methods for eigenvalue problems in partial differential equations, including adaptive and multigrid methods.

Finite Element Methods

Finite element methods, approximation theory, a posteriori error estimation and superconvergence analysis.

Scientific Computing

Scientific computing, numerical analysis and applied mathematics in general.
